تستخدمه لما \(P(k+1)\) تحتاج أكثر من \(P(k)\)، مثل متتابعة كل حد فيها يعتمد على الحدين اللي قبله.

The Strong Principle of Mathematical Induction. For each positive integer \(n\), let \(P(n)\) be a statement. If (1) \(P(1)\) is true and (2) for every positive integer \(k\), if \(P(i)\) is true for every integer \(i\) with \(1 \le i \le k\), then \(P(k+1)\) is true, then \(P(n)\) is true for every positive integer \(n\).

الفرق الوحيد عن العادي: تفترض صحة كل الحالات من 1 إلى k، مو حالة k بس. وفي المتتابعات: القيم الأولى المعطاة اسمها initial values، والقاعدة اسمها recurrence relation.

تفترض\(P(k)\) بس\(P(1), P(2), \ldots, P(k)\) كلها
تستخدمه لما\(P(k+1)\) تحتاج اللي قبلها مباشرة\(P(k+1)\) تحتاج حالتين أو أكثر، أو حالة أبعد

Exam question. A sequence \(\{a_n\}\) is defined recursively by \(a_1 = 1\), \(a_2 = 3\) and \(a_n = 2a_{n-1} - a_{n-2}\) for \(n \ge 3\). Then \(a_n = 2n - 1\) for all \(n \in \mathbb{N}\).

Base
Since \(a_1 = 2 \cdot 1 - 1 = 1\), the formula holds for \(n = 1\).
Assume
Assume for an arbitrary positive integer \(k\) that \(a_i = 2i - 1\) for all integers \(i\) with \(1 \le i \le k\).
Show
We show that \(a_{k+1} = 2(k+1) - 1 = 2k + 1\).
Small k
If \(k = 1\), then \(a_{k+1} = a_2 = 3 = 2 \cdot 1 + 1\). So we may assume that \(k \ge 2\).
Step
Since \(k + 1 \ge 3\), \(\;a_{k+1} = 2a_k - a_{k-1} = 2(2k - 1) - (2k - 3) = 2k + 1\).
Conclude
By the Strong Principle of Mathematical Induction, \(a_n = 2n - 1\) for all \(n \in \mathbb{N}\).
سطر Small k عليه درجة:عند \(k = 1\) الحد \(a_2\) قيمة ابتدائية، والعلاقة التكرارية تبدأ من \(n = 3\)، و \(a_{k-1} = a_0\) مو موجود. فتثبتها من القيمة المعطاة.

Example 8 (slides). For each integer \(n \ge 8\), there are nonnegative integers \(a\) and \(b\) such that \(n = 3a + 5b\).

Base
Since \(8 = 3 \cdot 1 + 5 \cdot 1\), the statement is true for \(n = 8\).
Assume
Assume for each integer \(i\) with \(8 \le i \le k\), where \(k \ge 8\), that there are nonnegative integers \(s\) and \(t\) such that \(i = 3s + 5t\).
Show
We show that \(k + 1 = 3x + 5y\) for some nonnegative integers \(x\) and \(y\).
Small k
Since \(9 = 3 \cdot 3 + 5 \cdot 0\) and \(10 = 3 \cdot 0 + 5 \cdot 2\), this is true if \(k + 1 = 9\) or \(k + 1 = 10\). So we may assume that \(k + 1 \ge 11\).
Step
Then \(8 \le (k+1) - 3 \lt k\). By the induction hypothesis, there are nonnegative integers \(a\) and \(b\) such that \((k+1) - 3 = 3a + 5b\), and so \(k + 1 = 3(a+1) + 5b\). Letting \(x = a + 1\) and \(y = b\) gives the result.
Conclude
By the Strong Principle of Mathematical Induction, the statement holds for every integer \(n \ge 8\).

(a)

Answer
\(F_1 = F_2 = 1\) and \(F_n = F_{n-1} + F_{n-2}\) for every integer \(n \ge 3\).

(b)

Setup
For each \(n \in \mathbb{N}\), let \(P(n)\): \(F_n\) is even if and only if \(3 \mid n\). We use the Strong Principle of Mathematical Induction.
Base
\(F_1 = 1\) is odd and \(3 \nmid 1\), so \(P(1)\) is true.
Assume
Assume for an arbitrary positive integer \(k\) that \(P(i)\) is true for every integer \(i\) with \(1 \le i \le k\).
Show
We show that \(P(k+1)\) is true, that is, \(F_{k+1}\) is even if and only if \(3 \mid (k+1)\).
Small k
If \(k = 1\), then \(F_{k+1} = F_2 = 1\) is odd and \(3 \nmid 2\), so \(P(2)\) is true. So we may assume that \(k \ge 2\).
Step
Since \(k + 1 \ge 3\), \(F_{k+1} = F_k + F_{k-1}\). Exactly one of the integers \(k-1\), \(k\), \(k+1\) is divisible by \(3\).
Case 1. \(3 \mid (k+1)\). Then \(3 \nmid k\) and \(3 \nmid (k-1)\), so \(F_k\) and \(F_{k-1}\) are odd by the hypothesis. Hence \(F_{k+1}\) is even.
Case 2. \(3 \nmid (k+1)\). Then exactly one of \(k\) and \(k-1\) is divisible by \(3\), so exactly one of \(F_k\) and \(F_{k-1}\) is even. Hence \(F_{k+1}\) is odd.
Therefore \(F_{k+1}\) is even if and only if \(3 \mid (k+1)\).
Conclude
By the Strong Principle of Mathematical Induction, \(2 \mid F_n\) if and only if \(3 \mid n\), for every \(n \in \mathbb{N}\).
ليش سطر Small k؟\(F_{k+1}\) تعتمد على \(F_k\) و \(F_{k-1}\)، فالعلاقة تشتغل بس لما \(k \ge 2\). لما \(k = 1\) تتحقق من \(F_2\) مباشرة. وحالة \(n = 3\) تطلع من الخطوة نفسها لما \(k = 2\).
