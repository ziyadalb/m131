Q1: Prove or disprove the following:15 marks
1. For every positive integer \(n\), \(\;1 \cdot 2 + 2 \cdot 3 + \cdots + n(n+1) = \dfrac{n(n+1)(n+2)}{3}\). (4 marks)

4. A sequence \(\{a_n\}\) is defined recursively by \(a_1 = 2\), \(a_2 = 4\) and \(a_n = a_{n-1} + 2a_{n-2}\) for \(n \ge 3\). Then \(a_n = 2^n\) for all \(n \in \mathbb{N}\). (4 marks)
5. If a set \(A\) of real numbers has a least element, then \(A\) has a unique least element. (2 marks)

حلول الاختبار القصير1 و2

1. True

Base
Since \(1 \cdot 2 = 2 = \frac{1 \cdot 2 \cdot 3}{3}\), the formula holds for \(n = 1\).
Assume
Assume that \(1 \cdot 2 + \cdots + k(k+1) = \frac{k(k+1)(k+2)}{3}\) for a positive integer \(k\).
Step
\(1 \cdot 2 + \cdots + (k+1)(k+2) = \frac{k(k+1)(k+2)}{3} + (k+1)(k+2) = (k+1)(k+2)\left(\frac{k}{3} + 1\right) = \frac{(k+1)(k+2)(k+3)}{3}.\)
Conclude
By the Principle of Mathematical Induction, the formula holds for every positive integer \(n\).

2. True

Base
When \(n = 1\), \(6^1 - 1 = 5\) and \(5 \mid 5\).
Assume
Assume that \(5 \mid (6^k - 1)\). Then \(6^k - 1 = 5x\) for some integer \(x\), so \(6^k = 5x + 1\).
Step
\(6^{k+1} - 1 = 6 \cdot 6^k - 1 = 6(5x + 1) - 1 = 30x + 5 = 5(6x + 1)\). Since \(6x + 1 \in \mathbb{Z}\), \(5 \mid (6^{k+1} - 1)\).
Conclude
By the Principle of Mathematical Induction, \(5 \mid (6^n - 1)\) for every positive integer \(n\).

حلول الاختبار القصير3 إلى 5

3. False

Answer
Let \(n = 1\). Then \(1! = 1\) and \(2^1 = 2\), so \(n! \gt 2^n\) is false. Hence \(n = 1\) is a counterexample. (The inequality is true only for \(n \ge 4\).)

لاحظ: عبارة False تاخذ درجتها كاملة بمثال واحد محسوب. لا تكتب برهان.

4. True

Base
Since \(a_1 = 2 = 2^1\), the formula holds for \(n = 1\).
Assume
Assume for an arbitrary positive integer \(k\) that \(a_i = 2^i\) for every integer \(i\) with \(1 \le i \le k\).
Show
We show that \(a_{k+1} = 2^{k+1}\).
Small k
If \(k = 1\), then \(a_{k+1} = a_2 = 4 = 2^2\). So we may assume that \(k \ge 2\).
Step
Since \(k + 1 \ge 3\), \(\;a_{k+1} = a_k + 2a_{k-1} = 2^k + 2 \cdot 2^{k-1} = 2^k + 2^k = 2^{k+1}\).
Conclude
By the Strong Principle of Mathematical Induction, \(a_n = 2^n\) for all \(n \in \mathbb{N}\).

5. True

Proof
Let \(m_1\) and \(m_2\) be least elements of \(A\). Since \(m_1\) is a least element and \(m_2 \in A\), \(m_2 \ge m_1\). Since \(m_2\) is a least element and \(m_1 \in A\), \(m_1 \ge m_2\). Therefore \(m_1 = m_2\).

القوالب

عادي:

Base \({P(1)}\) → Assume \({P(k)}\) → Show \({P(k+1)}\) → Step → By the PMI

عام (\(n \ge m\)): نفسه، بس الأساس \(P(m)\) و \(k \ge m\).

قوي:

Base → Assume \({P(i)}\) for \({1 \le i \le k}\) → Show → Small k → Step → By the Strong PMI

الحركة لكل نوع

مجموع: افصل الحد الأخير، وخذ \((k+1)\) عامل مشترك.

قسمة: اعزل القوة من الفرض \(a^k = dx + 1\) وعوّض.

متباينة: سلسلة من الطرف الأيسر، وكل > لها سبب، وافصل k الصغيرة.

متتابعة: كل القيم الابتدائية في الأساس + سطر Small k.

الفخاخ

  • جرّب \(n = 1, 2\) قبل أي برهان.
  • اقرأ المجموعة: positive من 1، nonnegative من 0.
  • \(4 \cdot 2^{2k} - 1 \ne 4(2^{2k} - 1)\).
  • Assume «for an arbitrary k» مو «for every k».
  • الخطوة بدون أساس ما تثبت شي.

الترتيب الحسن

  • \(\mathbb{N}\)، وأي مجموعة منتهية، وأي جزء من \(\mathbb{N}\): نعم.
  • \(\{i \in \mathbb{Z} : i \ge m\}\): نعم.
  • \([0, 1]\)، \((0, 1)\)، \(\mathbb{Z}\)، \(\mathbb{Q}^+\): لا.
  • لنفيها: جزئية وحدة بدون أصغر عنصر.
