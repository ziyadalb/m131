مثال 5 في السلايدات (ما انسأل للحين)

Result. For every nonnegative integer \(n\), \(9 \mid (4^{3n} - 1)\).

بالعربي: 9 يقسم \(4^{3n} - 1\) لكل n من 0 وفوق. كلمة nonnegative تعني إن البداية من 0.

بالأرقام: n = 1: 63 = 9 × 7 ✓. حركة القسمة: الفرض معادلة (\(4^{3k} = 9x + 1\))، وحد k + 1 من حد k (\(4^{3k+3} = 4^3 \cdot 4^{3k}\))، وعوّض، وطلّع 9 عامل مشترك.

نكتب (برهان السلايدات)

1الأساس
We proceed by induction. When \(n = 0\), \(4^{3n} - 1 = 0\). Since \(9 \mid 0\), the statement is true when \(n = 0\).البداية من 0، و 0 = 9 × 0.
2الفرض
Assume that \(9 \mid (4^{3k} - 1)\), where \(k\) is a nonnegative integer.
3المطلوب
We now show that \(9 \mid (4^{3k+3} - 1)\).عوّضنا n = k + 1: \(4^{3(k+1)} = 4^{3k+3}\).
4الخطوة
Since \(9 \mid (4^{3k} - 1)\), it follows that \(4^{3k} - 1 = 9x\) for some integer \(x\). Hence, \(4^{3k} = 9x + 1\). Now, observe that \[\begin{aligned} 4^{3k+3} - 1 &= 4^3 \cdot \hyp{4^{3k}} - 1 = 64\hyp{(9x + 1)} - 1 \\ &= 64 \cdot 9x + 64 - 1 = 64 \cdot 9x + 63 = 9(64x + 7). \end{aligned}\] Since \(64x + 7\) is an integer, \(9 \mid (4^{3k+3} - 1)\).عوّضنا \(4^{3k}\) نفسه (الأزرق)، مو العبارة كلها. و 63 = 9 × 7، فطلع 9 عامل مشترك.
5الختام
By the Principle of Mathematical Induction, \(9 \mid (4^{3n} - 1)\) for every nonnegative integer \(n\).اسم المبدأ كامل.

مثال الكتاب 6.11↩ السؤال

حل الكتاب
We proceed by induction. The result is true when \(n = 0\) since in this case \(2^{2n} - 1 = 0\) and \(3 \mid 0\). Assume that \(3 \mid (2^{2k} - 1)\), where \(k\) is a nonnegative integer. We show that \(3 \mid (2^{2k+2} - 1)\). Since \(3 \mid (2^{2k} - 1)\), there exists an integer \(x\) such that \(2^{2k} - 1 = 3x\) and so \(2^{2k} = 3x + 1\). Now, \[2^{2k+2} - 1 = 4 \cdot \hyp{2^{2k}} - 1 = 4\hyp{(3x + 1)} - 1 = 12x + 3 = 3(4x + 1).\] Since \(4x + 1\) is an integer, \(3 \mid (2^{2k+2} - 1)\). By the Principle of Mathematical Induction, \(3 \mid (2^{2n} - 1)\) for every nonnegative integer \(n\).نفس حركة مثال 5 بالضبط: \(2^{2k+2} = 4 \cdot 2^{2k}\).

تمرين 6.16↩ السؤال↩ البنك

1الأساس
When \(n = 1\), \(3^{4n+1} - 5^{2n-1} = 3^5 - 5 = 238 = 7 \cdot 34\), so \(7 \mid 238\).
2الفرض
Assume that \(7 \mid (3^{4k+1} - 5^{2k-1})\) for a positive integer \(k\). Then \(3^{4k+1} - 5^{2k-1} = 7x\) for some integer \(x\), so \(3^{4k+1} = 7x + 5^{2k-1}\).نكتب الفرض معادلة، ونعزل الحد اللي فيه 3.
3المطلوب
We show that \(7 \mid (3^{4k+5} - 5^{2k+1})\).
4الخطوة
\[\begin{aligned} 3^{4k+5} - 5^{2k+1} &= 81 \cdot \hyp{3^{4k+1}} - 25 \cdot 5^{2k-1} = 81\hyp{\left(7x + 5^{2k-1}\right)} - 25 \cdot 5^{2k-1} \\ &= 567x + 56 \cdot 5^{2k-1} = 7\left(81x + 8 \cdot 5^{2k-1}\right). \end{aligned}\] Since \(81x + 8 \cdot 5^{2k-1}\) is an integer, \(7 \mid (3^{4k+5} - 5^{2k+1})\).\(3^{4k+5} = 3^4 \cdot 3^{4k+1}\) و \(5^{2k+1} = 5^2 \cdot 5^{2k-1}\). و 567 = 7 × 81، و 56 = 7 × 8.
5الختام
By the Principle of Mathematical Induction, \(7 \mid (3^{4n+1} - 5^{2n-1})\) for every positive integer \(n\).

توأم: 5 يقسم \(6^n - 1\)↩ السؤال

الحل
When \(n = 1\), \(6^1 - 1 = 5\) and \(5 \mid 5\). Assume that \(5 \mid (6^k - 1)\) for a positive integer \(k\). Then \(6^k - 1 = 5x\) for some integer \(x\), so \(6^k = 5x + 1\). We show that \(5 \mid (6^{k+1} - 1)\). Now, \(6^{k+1} - 1 = 6 \cdot \hyp{6^k} - 1 = 6\hyp{(5x + 1)} - 1 = 30x + 5 = 5(6x + 1)\). Since \(6x + 1\) is an integer, \(5 \mid (6^{k+1} - 1)\). By the Principle of Mathematical Induction, \(5 \mid (6^n - 1)\) for every positive integer \(n\).
